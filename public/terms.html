The service

0node.io is a non-custodial web interface to Solana's native stake program. It lets you delegate SOL from your own wallet to the 0node validator (or, optionally, to any other Solana validator) and manage the resulting stake accounts.

We do not custody, hold, lend, or rehypothecate user funds. Every transaction is built locally in your browser and signed inside your own wallet. Your private key never reaches us.

Validator commission

The 0node validator currently charges a 5% commission on staking rewards, plus its share of Jito MEV tips per the validator configuration on-chain. Commission is set in the validator's on-chain vote account and may change with on-chain notice. You can always verify the current value via any Solana explorer.
