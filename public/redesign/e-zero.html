Non-custodial by construction

Your wallet creates the stake account. Your wallet signs every transaction. Your wallet is the only authority. We can't move your funds, change your delegation, or freeze your account. There is no contract between us — only the Solana protocol.

Open and auditable

The frontend source is on GitHub. The validator's identity and commission are visible on-chain. Builds are reproducible via Bun + Vite. There are no closed components, no hidden fees, no opaque rewards distribution. What you see is what runs.
