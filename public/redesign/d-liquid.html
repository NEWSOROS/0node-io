Built on the primitive

Your stake account is created and delegated using Solana's built-in StakeProgram. There is no proxy contract, no liquid staking token, and no admin key. The validator authority and withdraw authority are both your wallet — we are not on the signature path.

  • Non-custodial by construction
  • Compatible with Phantom, Solflare, Backpack
  • Frontend source on GitHub, reproducible build

Jito BAM included

Our validator runs the Jito-Solana BAM client. Tips and MEV rewards captured each block are added to the staker payout per epoch, after our 5% commission. We don't run separate liquid-staking pools or charge for "stake-pool" wrappers.

  • Jito tip flow → epoch payout
  • Visible on Stakewiz, Validators.app
  • Commission set on-chain, audit anytime
